BRF Privacy Policy

Urban Army AB ("we", "us", or "our") publishes BRF. This page explains how we handle your data – the short version is: we don't collect any.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. BRF does not require an internet connection, a user account, or login credentials, and it does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or any other personal information on your device.

Third-Party Services

BRF does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No data is shared with third parties.

Children's Privacy

BRF does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children. Because no personal information is collected from any user, no special data handling is required for children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

Changes to This Policy

BRF is in development. If a future version collects any data, we will update this Privacy Policy before that version is released. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
