LumaLog

Real wood. Living light. A real wood log with a warm light hidden inside. Stand it with your other logs and an unused fireplace glows like a fire — no flame, no heat and no cables.

The Idea

Flame lamps look fine until you see the lamp. Most fireplace lights are a bulb or a screen sitting between the logs, and your eye goes straight to it. LumaLog hides the light inside a log and aims it at everything around it — the other logs, the hearth floor and the back wall.

What you see is warm light moving over real surfaces, the way a fire actually looks. The fireplace feels alive again, even if it hasn't been lit in years.

How It Works

A Real Log Cut from real wood and hollowed out from the back. From the front it's just one more log in the stack.
Light You Don't See Up to six hidden light zones point at the logs around it and the hearth floor, so the glow moves across wood and brick instead of shining at you.
No Flame, No Heat, No Cables A rechargeable battery inside, charged over USB‑C. Switch it on with the button underneath, or from your phone.
A Fire That Doesn't Loop The flicker is modelled on a real fire — a slow swell, a quicker flicker and the odd flare — and never repeats in a pattern you can spot.
The App Fire, Ember and Flame modes, brightness, light zones, auto‑off and the crackle of a real fire from your phone. Over Bluetooth, no account.
Match Your Wood The first LumaLog is birch. Beech, oak and pine are planned, so it blends in with the wood you already keep by the fire.

Where It Stands

LumaLog is in prototype: we're building and testing the first logs now. There's no price yet, and battery figures will be published once they're measured, not before. Join the waitlist on getlumalog.com and you'll hear first when it can be ordered.

LumaLog is for cold fireplaces only. It's a light, not firewood — never put it in a fire or on hot embers.
