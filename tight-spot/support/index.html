Questions

I can't feel the pieces.
On iPhone, a pinched piece pushes back through vibrations. Check that System Haptics is on in Settings → Sounds & Haptics. iPad has no vibration, so there the piece shows its pressure by lagging behind your finger.
When do I lose a piece?
If the spring shifts a little while you take a piece out, you lose that piece. If it shifts a lot, you also pay the piece's value in points. Let go before the piece is out to pass instead.
Can we play on separate phones?
No. Pass-and-play is for 2 to 6 players taking turns on one device, so no network is needed.
