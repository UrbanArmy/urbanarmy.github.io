Tight Spot: Steady Hands

The pieces are packed tight, and a spring presses on all of them. Some are loose. Some are pinched. Your job: take one out without moving anything else.

Drag a piece slowly upward and feel it. A loose piece slides free; a pinched one holds back and pushes against your hand. Keep pulling and risk it, or let go and pass. Play solo, against the computer, or pass the device around with up to six friends, in five themes that are all free.

No account, no ads and no tracking. Nothing you do in the game leaves your device.
