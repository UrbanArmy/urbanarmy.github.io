Fredrik

Rich in peace, like the name. Fredrik goes back to Old High German fridu, “peace”, and rīhhi, “mighty, ruler” – and the ending is the same old word as rich.

A small app about the name in nine languages, where it always carries that language’s form: Fredrik, Frederick, Friedrich, Frédéric, Federico. Count down to the name day, meet a famous namesake every day, read about the name, its relatives and its kings, breathe a minute of peace, take the quiz and share your own certificate.

No account, no ads and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app leaves your phone.
