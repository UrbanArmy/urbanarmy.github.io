Questions

Why does the name day differ between languages?
The app follows the language you use. The Norwegian name day is 14 November; in the other languages it is 18 July, the feast of Frederick of Utrecht and the Swedish name day.
How do I change the language?
Open More → Language. By default the app follows your phone's language.
Reminders don't arrive.
Check that notifications are allowed for Fredrik in the iPhone Settings app.
