The app in short

The Gogolis app is simple play for children roughly aged one to four, built around the everyday routines the songs are about. It works with no internet connection, collects nothing, stores nothing on the device, shows no ads and has nothing to buy inside it. There are no accounts and no sign-in. The only link out of the app is to the privacy policy, behind a question for a grown-up.
