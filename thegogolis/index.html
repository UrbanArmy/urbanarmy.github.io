The Gogolis

Six small, funny, huggable characters who sing children through the ordinary moments of a day — brushing teeth, taking a bath, tidying up, eating vegetables, going somewhere new. Made for ages 1–4.

Visit the Gogolis Watch on YouTube

About the Gogolis

Routine songs are one of the few genuinely useful things a screen can do for a toddler: they turn a transition — "we are tidying now" — into something with a beginning and an end. The Gogolis are built around that idea. Every song is short, calm, and meant to be played at the actual moment of the task, not watched for its own sake.

The music and animation are AI‑generated; the lyrics are written by hand. The cast is fixed at six, each with one letter and one colour, held to a character bible so they stay themselves from one song to the next.

Meet the Cast

Gambo G · Red. The one who eats the most and moves the fastest. All‑in, immediate, and delighted about it.
Poff P · Orange. Round, springy, never quite still — and usually carrying something far too big.
Nibs N · Yellow. The one who is not sure about this. Tries it anyway, and likes it.
Lilly L · Green. The one with the plan that includes everybody. Turns a chore into a group game.
Toti T · Cyan. The one looking at everything, and photographing it. Wants the group in the picture.
Mimi M · Pink. The one who floats, glows, and changes colour with whatever they eat.

Where to Find Them

New songs land on YouTube first. The full catalogue is also on Spotify and Apple Music for audio‑only listening in the car, the pram, or at bedtime. Behind the scenes, for grown‑ups, is Instagram.

For Grown‑Ups

No Ads, No Tracking Nothing on thegogolis.com is collected, measured, or sold. No sign‑up, no accounts, no cookies.
Made for Kids Every video is designated Made for Kids on YouTube — no personalised ads and no comments.
Short and Calm One to three minutes each. Nothing overstimulating, nothing that begs for attention or money.
No Villains No jeopardy and no meanness. The strongest conflict is mild reluctance, and it always resolves.
